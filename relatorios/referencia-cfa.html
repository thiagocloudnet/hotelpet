CFA Recognized Breeds

Abyssinian
Abyssinian

Coat Length: Shorthair

Characteristics: Ticked coat; ruddy, cinnamon, blue and fawn colors

Personality: Busy, active, agenda-driven and affectionate
American Bobtail
American Bobtail

Coat Length: Longhair and Shorthair

Characteristics: Medium to large, naturally occurring, bobtailed cat

Personality: Loving and intelligent
American Curl
American Curl

Coat Length: Longhair and Shorthair

Characteristics: Ears curl back, away from the face; available in a variety of colors and patterns

Personality: Energetic and affectionate
American Shorthair
American Shorthair

Coat Length: Shorthair

Characteristics: Stocky, working breed: available in a wide variety of colors and patterns

Personality: Even temperament
American Wirehair
American Wirehair

Coat Length: Shorthair

Characteristics: Crimped, springy coat; available in a variety of colors and patterns

Personality: Even temperament
Balinese
Balinese

Coat Length: Longhair

Characteristics: Longhaired variety of the Siamese and Colorpoint Shorthair

Personality: Vocal, affectionate, active
Bengal
Bengal

Coat Length: Shorthair

Characteristics: Richly colored, highly contrasted coat of vivid spots or distinctive marbling

Personality: Curious and athletic
Birman
Birman

Coat Length: Longhair

Characteristics: Stocky body with white feet and point color pattern; easy to groom coat of intermediate length

Personality: Sweet and affectionate
Bombay
Bombay

Coat Length: Shorthair

Characteristics: Glossy, black coat

Personality: Playful and affectionate; lap cats
British Shorthair
British Shorthair

Coat Length: Shorthair

Characteristics: Stocky, sturdy, plush coat; blue is very popular but also comes in other colors

Personality: Calm and quiet; enjoy people
Burmese
Burmese

Coat Length: Shorthair

Characteristics: Stocky and well muscled; sable, also champagne, blue and platinum

Personality: People oriented, affectionate
Burmilla
Burmilla

Coat Length: Longhair and Shorthair

Characteristics: Sparkling silver coat, and distinctive “make up” lining the nose, lips and eyes

Personality: Sociable, playful, and affectionate
